Delivery records

Each delivery has immutable plan.json, append-only ledger.jsonl, evidence, and one immutable landing.receipt.json. Plans serialize ordered refs, invariants, conflict decisions, regeneration, gates, STOP or ASK items, allowed destination, and push posture.

A landed receipt needs distinct verifier and integrator identities, passed independent verification, gate refs, before and after commits, and passed remote equality. Project OS records the protocol; adapters execute product-specific Git, deployment, and rollback commands.

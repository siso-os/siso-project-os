Sprints

A sprint coordinates canonical task IDs through sprint.json, ledger.jsonl, lane artifacts, and evidence.

SPRINT-YYYY-MM-DD-slug/
  sprint.json
  ledger.jsonl
  lanes/<lane>/brief.packet.json
  lanes/<lane>/state.json
  lanes/<lane>/log.jsonl
  lanes/<lane>/returns/
  evidence/

Starting freezes membership. Lane write fences may overlap only through explicit sequencing. Successful close requires passed lanes and receipted required gates; archive is a separate terminal move.

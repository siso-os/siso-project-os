Currency and proof

Current, draft, unverified, stale, and historical are semantic states. Evidence grade, source revision, dependency paths, observed time, and verifier make claims reviewable. Detection reports decay without rewriting authored truth.

Joined anti-amnesia

Generated HTML/JSON may join current docs, decisions, proof, runs, research gaps/dead ends, memory, capabilities, resume snapshots, and verifier/landing receipts. Every section is bounded and routes to canonical records.

Determinism

Projection inputs are ordered repository-relative paths and canonical bytes. Builders exclude wall clocks, local databases, network state, absolute paths, and modification time. Checks compute expected bytes in memory and never repair.
